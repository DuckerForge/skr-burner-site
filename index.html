    How the games work

    Every rule, in plain words

    The same numbers the app shows before you tap. Odds and prizes are read from the live config, never hidden.

    🔥 Weekly Giveaway

    • Burn at least once in the week and you are in. Burn more, rank higher.
    • Every burn, mint, claim and snipe feeds the pool: 20% of burn fees, 12.5% of mining fees, 2% of every Flag Wars snipe.
    • Monday 00:00 Rome: 5 winners, picked from the top-10 burners and at random. The pool minus a 0.1 SOL rollover is split equally.
    • Paid automatically on-chain within 15 minutes. Signature on the Ranks tab.

    🎟️ Monthly Draw

    • 1 SKR burned this month = 1 ticket. Mining counts, every burn counts. Tickets reset on the 1st.
    • The pot is a real wallet: 30% of burn fees, 20% of claim fees, 10% of mining fees, 3% of snipes and part of every Fire Vault entry land there.
    • Drawn on the 1st at 00:10 UTC: 5 winners, random, weighted by tickets. Prize = pot minus 0.1 SOL reserve, capped at 5 SOL, split equally.
    • Winners tap CLAIM in the app within 7 days. Unclaimed SOL stays in the wallet and rolls into next month.

    🏴 Flag Wars

    • Pay SOL and burn SKR to steal the flag. Price starts at 0.05 SOL every Monday and climbs ×1.5, then ×1.2, then ×1.15 per snipe.
    • Of every snipe: 65% grows the bag, 25% is paid on the spot to the player you stole from, 2% weekly, 3% monthly, 5% dev.
    • Hold the flag to farm Fire Points every hour, boosted on Monday and Thursday.
    • Whoever holds it at Monday reset takes the bag minus 0.05 SOL rollover, paid automatically.

    🟢 Fire Vault

    • 0.1 SOL per try, 3 tries a day. 90% of every entry goes into the pool, and the pool is the prize.
    • Base odds 3%, plus boosts: burn $1 of SKR, open a chest with SKR, snipe the flag, own an NFT. Up to 8%.
    • Win = 40% of the pool, sent to your wallet by the server. Fire Keys open it free at half odds.
    • The roll happens server-side after your payment is verified on-chain. The app never decides.

    🌋 Magma Vault

    • 0.5 SOL per try, 3 tries a day. 80% goes into the pool, 5% feeds the Fire Vault.
    • Same 3% base odds and the same boosts, up to 8%. Your boosts are shown before you open.
    • Win = 40% of the pool, never less than 0.5 SOL when the wallet has it, capped at 50 SOL.

    🟡 SKR Chest

    • $2 worth of SKR per try (price read live), 3 tries a day. 90% into the pool, 1% burned.
    • Odds 6%, win 40% of the SKR pool. SKR Keys drop from Daily Fire Chests and open it free at half odds.

    🪙 Coin Flip PvP

    • Pick FIRE or WATER and a stake. Your challenge sits in the lobby up to 2 hours, another Seeker takes the other side.
    • The server commits a hash before anyone joins and reveals the seed after: the result is sha256(seed + join signature). Anyone can verify it.
    • Winner gets both stakes. A 3% fee on the pot keeps the lights on. Cancel any time before someone joins.

    🎁 Daily Drop & Fire Chests

    • Daily Drop: burned at least 1 SKR in the last 7 days? Claim 1 to 5 SKR every day, from a pool fed by chest rerolls. You sign, you keep the account.
    • Daily Fire Chests: 3 free opens a day for Fire Points, reroll with SKR up to 5 times. 1 chest in 250 drops a Fire Key.
    • Airdrop Pt.2: 121,168 SKR, split by Fire Points rank between the top 5 and one random burner.

    • Entertainment only. Prizes come from what players have already contributed. No outcome is promised, no return is implied and nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice. SKR and SOL are volatile and can go to zero.
    • Where it is not offered. The reward features are not offered to anyone in a country, state or territory where such features are restricted, licensed or prohibited, and are void where prohibited. Checking your local law is your responsibility. You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live.
    • On-chain is final. Burns are irreversible and transactions cannot be refunded. Odds and prizes are shown in the app before you confirm, every roll happens server-side after your payment is verified on Solana, and every payout has a public signature.
    • Non-custodial. Your keys never leave your Seeker. Nobody at DuckerForge can move your tokens, and nobody will ever ask for your seed phrase.
