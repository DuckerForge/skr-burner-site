Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What we collect

  • Your public wallet address, when you connect.
  • The coins you launch: name, ticker, description, links and image. These are public by design, like the token itself.
  • Public blockchain data about trades of SeekerLaunch coins, to show the live feed and run the Launch Jackpot.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or location, and we never see your private keys.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show coins and trades, decide badges and jackpot winners, prevent abuse (for example rate limits on launches).

Where it is stored

Google Firebase and Google Cloud Storage. Coin images and metadata are public files so that wallets and explorers can display your coin.

Third parties

The app talks to the Solana blockchain through Helius RPC, to the Meteora program and, for graduated coins, to Jupiter. Their own policies apply to those services.

Your choices

You can disconnect your wallet at any time. Data written on the blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone. For anything else, write to infernaytb@gmail.com.
