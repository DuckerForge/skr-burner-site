Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

SeekerLaunch is built to need as little data as possible. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location, and we never see your private keys.

What we process

  • Your public wallet address, when you connect your wallet, to build your transactions and show your coins, rewards and badges.
  • The coins you launch: name, ticker, description, links, card style and image. They are public by design, like the token itself, and are stored on our servers and in the token's public metadata.
  • Public blockchain data about trades of SeekerLaunch coins (wallet, side, amount, time), to show the live feed and charts and to run the jackpot and badges.
  • Your .skr name: if your wallet owns a .skr domain, we read it from the public Solana blockchain and show it in the live feed instead of your address.
  • Whether your wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token, read from the blockchain, to mark coins made on a Seeker.

On your phone only

While you prepare a coin, the app saves the draft (image, name, texts, style) on your phone so you do not lose it. It never leaves your phone until you press Launch, and it is deleted after a successful launch. The app also remembers which wallet you connected and your language.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show coins and trades, build transactions for you to sign, decide badges and jackpot winners, and prevent abuse (for example rate limits on launches). We do not sell data, we do not show ads and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud (database, server functions, image hosting), in the EU and US.
  • Helius and other Solana RPC providers, to read the blockchain and send transactions.
  • Jupiter, for prices and for trades after a coin graduates.
  • Meteora, the on-chain program where coins live.

These providers process technical data such as IP addresses as part of serving requests, under their own privacy policies.

Blockchain data cannot be deleted

Everything on the Solana blockchain (your coins, trades and wallet address) is public and permanent; nobody can delete it, including us.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access or delete the data we keep about your wallet on our servers (for example your cached .skr name and badge records) by writing to infernaytb@gmail.com, stating your wallet address. We answer within 30 days. Coins you launched can be hidden from the app on request.

Children

SeekerLaunch is for adults (18+) only and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Contact: infernaytb@gmail.com. SeekerLaunch is developed by DuckerForge.

SeekerLaunch 的设计原则是尽量少收集数据。我们不会索取你的姓名、邮箱、电话、联系人或位置，也永远看不到你的私钥。

我们处理的数据

  • 你的公开钱包地址：在你连接钱包时使用，用于构建交易并显示你的币、奖励和徽章。
  • 你发行的币：名称、代号、描述、链接、卡片样式和图片。它们和代币本身一样是公开的，保存在我们的服务器和代币的公开元数据中。
  • 公开的区块链数据：SeekerLaunch 币的交易（钱包、方向、金额、时间），用于实时动态、图表、奖池和徽章。
  • 你的 .skr 名称：如果你的钱包拥有 .skr 域名，我们会从公开的 Solana 区块链读取，并在动态中代替你的地址显示。
  • 你的钱包是否持有 Seeker Genesis Token：从区块链读取，用于标记在 Seeker 上创建的币。

仅保存在你的手机上

你准备发行币时，应用会把草稿（图片、名称、文字、样式）保存在手机上，以免丢失。在你点击发行之前它不会离开你的手机，发行成功后即被删除。应用还会记住你连接的钱包和语言。

我们如何使用

仅用于运行应用：显示币和交易、为你构建需签名的交易、确定徽章和奖池获奖者，以及防止滥用（例如发行次数限制）。我们不出售数据、不展示广告，也不会跨应用或网站追踪你。

服务提供商

  • Google Firebase 和 Google Cloud（数据库、服务器函数、图片托管），位于欧盟和美国。
  • Helius 及其他 Solana RPC 提供商，用于读取区块链和发送交易。
  • Jupiter，用于价格以及毕业后的交易。
  • Meteora，币所在的链上程序。

这些提供商在处理请求时会依据其各自的隐私政策处理 IP 地址等技术数据。

区块链数据无法删除

Solana 区块链上的一切（你的币、交易和钱包地址）都是公开且永久的；包括我们在内，任何人都无法删除。

利用目的

アプリの運営のみに使います：コインと取引の表示、署名する取引の作成、バッジとジャックポット当選者の決定、不正防止（発行回数の制限など）。データの販売、広告表示、アプリやサイトをまたいだ追跡は行いません。

  • Jupiter：価格と卒業後の取引。
  • Meteora：コインが存在するオンチェーンプログラム。

これらの委託先は、リクエストの処理に伴い IP アドレスなどの技術データを各社のポリシーに従って処理します。

ブロックチェーンのデータは削除できません
