1. What SeekerLaunch is

SeekerLaunch is a software interface for the Solana Seeker that lets you create tokens on a bonding curve run by the public Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve program, and buy or sell them. SeekerLaunch does not issue, endorse or guarantee any token created by its users.

2. Non-custodial

You keep full control of your wallet. Transactions are prepared by our servers and signed by you in your wallet. We never have access to your private keys or your funds.

3. Fees

  • Launch fee: 0.02 SOL per coin, paid in the launch transaction.
  • Bonding curve trading fee: 1% of each trade, split between the Meteora protocol, the coin's creator and SeekerLaunch.
  • App fee: 0.5% on buys and sells made in the app.
  • Badge NFT mint: 0.005 SOL plus network rent.

Every fee is shown in your wallet before you sign.

6. Launch Jackpot and badges

The weekly Launch Jackpot is funded by a share of SeekerLaunch fees, not by players. Winners are chosen by our server with published rules and paid on-chain. Badge NFTs are collectibles with no promise of value.
