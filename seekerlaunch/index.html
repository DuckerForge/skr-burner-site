Launch your coin in 10 seconds

SeekerLaunch is a memecoin launchpad for the Solana Seeker phone. Snap a photo, pick a name and a ticker, sign once: your coin is live on Solana.

  • Your own coin on a Meteora bonding curve, graduating to a Meteora pool with locked liquidity
  • Creators earn a share of every trade of their coin, claimable any time
  • Live feed of every buy and sell, King of the Hill, weekly Launch Jackpot
  • Collectible badge NFTs for launching, buying and holding
Non-custodial. SeekerLaunch never holds your keys or your funds. Every transaction is built in the open, shown in your wallet and signed by you on your phone. Memecoins are highly volatile and can lose all their value: only use money you can afford to lose. Not financial advice.
