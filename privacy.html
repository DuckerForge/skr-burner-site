Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SKR Burner ("we", "us", DuckerForge) handles data. We prioritize your anonymity and the decentralized nature of the Solana blockchain.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as names, emails, or phone numbers. To provide leaderboard, giveaway, notifications, and game features, we process:

  • Public Wallet Addresses: Your Solana public key is used to track burn amounts, NFT ownership, Flag Wars activity, and rewards.
  • Transaction Metadata: Amount of $SKR burned, SOL paid for minting or Flag Wars snipes, and related on-chain signatures.
  • Firebase Database: We store partially masked wallet addresses, burn statistics, push tokens, language preference, and game/reward state in a Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Device push tokens: If you enable notifications, an FCM device token is stored so we can send optional alerts (pool milestones, weekly events, Flag Wars boost days, check-in reminders).

2. Data Transparency

  • On-Chain Data: All transactions are public on the Solana blockchain and can be viewed via explorers such as Solscan.
  • Leaderboard: Your wallet is masked (e.g., Abc1***xyz9) to provide a layer of privacy while maintaining competitive transparency.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to run the dApp:

  • Helius RPC: To interact with the Solana network.
  • Firebase: Leaderboard data, statistics, and push delivery.
  • CoinGecko API: Real-time price data for $SKR and SOL.
  • Pinata (IPFS): NFT metadata and images.
  • Jupiter / Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter: Swaps and wallet connection flows initiated by you.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The SKR Burner dApp does not use advertising cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics such as Google Analytics. This marketing website may use standard browser storage only as needed for basic functionality.

5. Your Choices

  • You can disable push notifications in the app or at the OS level at any time.
  • Disconnecting your wallet stops further authenticated writes from that session; historical on-chain activity remains public on Solana.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, reach out via our official X profile @SKRBurner or the contact channels on the SKR Burner site.
