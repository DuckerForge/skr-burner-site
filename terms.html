1. The Burn Mechanism

  • Irreversibility: You acknowledge that burning $SKR tokens is a permanent and irreversible action. Once tokens are sent to the burn address or processed via the burn instruction, they cannot be recovered.
  • No Financial Guarantee: Burning tokens is a community-driven deflationary action. It does not guarantee any increase in the value of your remaining tokens.

2. NFT Minting and Revenue Split

  • Cost: Minting an NFT requires a payment in SOL based on the selected Tier (Low Flame, Medium Heat, On Fire, or Legend).
  • Revenue Distribution: All SOL collected from NFT minting is automatically split:
    • 60% (Community Pool): Dedicated to weekly giveaways, prizes, and leaderboard rewards.
    • 40% (Dev & Ops): Allocated for server maintenance, RPC node costs, and app development.

3. Weekly Giveaway Rules

  • Eligibility: Users who interact with the burn or minting mechanisms may be entered into a weekly draw.
  • No Purchase Necessary (Compliance): Participation in the community is the primary driver. The giveaway is a promotional reward and not a regulated gambling product.
  • Selection: Winners are selected based on transparent community criteria or random on-chain/off-chain draws.

4. Flag Wars

  • Flag Wars is an optional in-app mini-game. Sniping the flag requires paying SOL (starting at 0.05 SOL and increasing with every successful steal: ×1.5 while the price is low, ×1.2 above 0.25 SOL, ×1.15 above 1 SOL) and burning a small amount of SKR.
  • Of each sniper payment: 65% goes to the Flag bag (community pool), 25% is paid on-chain to the previous flag holder in the same transaction, 2% to the Weekly Giveaway pool, 3% to Airdrop Pt.2 and 5% to development / operations. When there is no previous holder (first steal of the week) that 25% stays in the Flag bag.
  • Holding the flag accrues Fire Points over time (base rate, with higher rates on selected days). Hold rewards are credited when the flag is stolen or when the weekly reset settles.
  • The Monday reset awards the current flag holder the Flag bag prize (subject to rollover rules). Participation is voluntary and carries the same blockchain risks described below.

5. Fire Vault and SKR Chest

The Fire Vault and the SKR Chest are optional in-app features in which the outcome of each opening is determined by chance. They are not required in order to use the dApp, and no part of the dApp is conditional on participating in them.

  • Outcome determination: Each opening is resolved server-side by a single random draw performed at the moment the opening is processed, using a cryptographically seeded generator. The draw is recorded in an audit entry tied to that opening and is not repeated: an opening that has been drawn cannot be re-drawn, whether or not the participant reopens the application.
  • Fire Vault: entry of 0.1 SOL, up to three openings per participant per day. A winning opening pays 40% of the Vault pool as it stands at the moment of the draw. Openings that do not win award FirePoints only. Of each entry, 90% is added to the Vault pool, 2.5% to the Weekly Giveaway pool, 2.5% to the Airdrop Pt.2 pool and 5% to development and operations.
  • SKR Chest: entry of approximately USD 2 payable in SKR, up to three openings per participant per day. A winning opening pays 40% of the Chest pool as it stands at the moment of the draw. Of each entry, 90% is added to the Chest pool, 9% to development and operations, and 1% is irreversibly burned.
  • Stated probabilities: as at the date of this document, the probability of a winning opening is 5% for the Fire Vault and 10% for the SKR Chest. An opening funded by a Key (see below) is drawn at half the applicable probability. Each opening is drawn independently: previous outcomes do not affect subsequent ones, and no sequence of unsuccessful openings increases the probability of a subsequent one.
  • Keys: a Magic Key or SKR Key may be used to fund one of the participant's daily openings in place of the entry amount. A Key does not grant an additional opening beyond the daily limit, and an opening funded by a Key is drawn at half the probability stated above.
  • Variable prize: the prize is expressed as a percentage of a pool whose balance changes continuously with the activity of other participants. No prize amount is fixed, promised or guaranteed, and the amount displayed before an opening may differ from the amount payable at the moment of the draw.
  • Amendment of parameters: entry amounts, probabilities, prize percentages and daily limits may be amended at any time in order to maintain the balance of the features. The parameters in force are those displayed within the application at the time of the opening, which prevail over any figure stated in this document.
  • Finality: entry amounts are paid on-chain and are final. They are not refundable, and an opening that has been drawn cannot be reversed, cancelled or compensated, including where the outcome is unsuccessful.
  • FirePoints: FirePoints are a non-transferable in-app score with no monetary value. They are not redeemable for currency, tokens or goods, and confer no claim of any kind.
