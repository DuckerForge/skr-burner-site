Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Degen Chest App processes and why. Degen Chest is published and operated by CGroup ("we", "us") and developed by DuckerForge on behalf of CGroup. We designed the App to work without personal information.

1. What we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifiers. The App has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. What we process

DataWhy
Your public Solana wallet addressTo log you in (you sign a short login message in your wallet), verify your entry payments and send your prizes.
Your public .skr domain name, if you own oneShown instead of your address in the live feed, the King of the Hill and win notifications. It is read from the public Solana blockchain. Wallets without a .skr name are shown in shortened form (for example Abc1…xyz9).
Game recordsYour entries and their transaction signatures, outcomes, prizes, refunds, points, FREE chest and SKR drop history. We need them to run the game, prevent double use of a payment and handle support requests.
Push notification token, language and time zoneOnly if you allow notifications: to send you reminders (for example "your FREE chest is available") and big-win alerts in your language, at reasonable local hours.
IP addressProcessed only in memory to limit abusive request rates. It is not stored in our database.

On your device the App stores your session token and your settings (language, sound, vibration, graphics, session limit).

3. Public blockchain data

Every payment you make and every prize you receive is a Solana transaction. Transactions are public and permanent, and anyone can see them with a blockchain explorer. We cannot delete or change them.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging): game server, data storage and push notifications. The server and the database run in Google's europe-west1 region (Belgium, EU); Cloud Messaging is a global Google service.
  • Solana RPC providers: to read the blockchain and send transactions.
  • AllDomains name records, read from the Solana blockchain: to find your .skr name.
  • Price APIs (CoinGecko, Jupiter): to show SOL prices. Like any website, they receive standard request data such as your IP address, but no wallet or game data.
  • Your wallet app (through the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter): to connect and sign. We never see your private keys or seed phrase.

5. Retention

Game records are kept while the App operates and as long as needed to settle payments, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Push tokens are deleted when they stop working. Cached .skr names are refreshed every few days.

6. Your choices

  • You can turn notifications off in the App settings of Android at any time.
  • You can disconnect your wallet in Settings. This ends your session on that device.
  • You can ask us to delete the off-chain data linked to your wallet address (for example your push token and points) by writing to us. On-chain transactions cannot be deleted.

7. Children

The App is for adults only (18+). We do not knowingly process data of minors.
