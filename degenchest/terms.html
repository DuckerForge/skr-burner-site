Degen Chest is a game of chance played with real SOL. You can lose all the SOL you use to play. Only play with amounts you can afford to lose.

1. Eligibility

2. How the game works

  • Each chest has a price, a probability of winning and a prize range. They are always shown in the App before you open a chest.
  • To open a paid chest you sign a Solana transaction in your wallet. The outcome is then decided by our server with a cryptographically secure random number generator, at the moment your opening is processed. The outcome is stored before the App receives it and is never drawn again for the same entry.
  • After a win you can cash out or push. Each push multiplies the pot if it survives and loses the whole pot if it fails ("rug"). The probability of surviving each push is shown in the App. The maximum number of pushes can be limited so that every possible prize stays within the caps described below.
  • Prizes are sent automatically from the prize pool wallet to the wallet that paid the entry. You do not need to sign anything to receive them.

3. Fees and where your SOL goes

Every paid chest is split on-chain, in the same transaction you sign:

ShareDestination
80%Prize pool (pays the prizes)
10%Jackpots of all chests, in proportion to their price
10%Operator fee (CGroup)

You also pay the Solana network fee of your entry transaction (about 0.000005 SOL). The network fees of prizes, refunds and SKR drops are paid by us.

4. Jackpots

Each paid opening of a chest has a 1 in 800 chance to win that chest's entire jackpot, in addition to the normal outcome. Jackpots grow with 10% of every paid entry. The current amount of each jackpot is shown in the App.

5. Caps, availability and refunds

  • To protect the prize pool, we may limit the maximum prize of a single round, the total prizes paid per day and which chests are open. A chest is offered only when its full advertised prize range can be covered. Prizes are never reduced below the advertised range.
  • If you pay for a chest that cannot be opened at that moment (for example because it has just closed), your entry is refunded in full.
  • If a paid entry is never redeemed in the App (for example because the App was closed before the opening completed), it is refunded automatically about 21 to 24 hours after the payment.
  • If a payment to you fails because of a network problem, it is retried automatically. We use safeguards so that the same prize or refund is never paid twice.
  • If you win and leave the round open for 24 hours without cashing out or pushing, it is cashed out for you automatically at the level reached.

6. Points, FREE chests and the daily SKR drop

  • Points are an in-game counter. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and can be used only for FREE chests.
  • FREE chests cost 0 SOL. They are available only to wallets that opened a paid chest in the previous 24 hours, are limited per day, and are subject to a daily budget that may run out.
  • The daily SKR drop is a promotion. One paid chest unlocks 7 days of daily drops, one claim per UTC day. Each drop is 1 to 5 SKR, with the probabilities shown in the App. Drops depend on the availability of SKR and may be paused or limited.
  • We may change, suspend or end FREE chests, points and promotions at any time. Promotions are offered "as available".
